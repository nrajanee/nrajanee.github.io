I've made time lately to really just sit with my thoughts and reflect. Lots of
reading and thinking. Lots of yapping with friends. I was so inspired at MIT
yesterday too. Here's what I think:

1. If you're privileged — in most ways: monetarily, emotional support, love —
you owe it to the universe to take more risks, be outrageously kind and slightly
delusional, so that you can open doors for the less privileged.

2. There are a few things that truly matter, and I'll say this a million times:
sleep, food, curiosity, accountability, dancing, consistency, singing, nature, a
small change, love, kisses and hugs.

3. Inspired by MIT and my lovely friend: there's something very compelling about
learning about a person's process, their driving forces, and the different kinds
of environments that allow creativity, failure and excellence.

4. *Main Wapaas Aaunga* is a beautiful movie — kindness and love in the middle of
horror. My grandma and I watched it in the theater a while ago and we both loved
it.

5. Parents have your best interest in mind, but they also don't have all the data
points (you do) to make the decision for you. Same goes for other family and
friends.

6. Decentering men has been the greatest thing Gen Z women have done for
themselves, and that's scary for everyone — including our parents, because we're
not getting married to men who aren't aligned with us.
